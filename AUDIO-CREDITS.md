# Bird recordings

The U.S. National Park Service recorded these birds and distributes the original MP3s in the public domain through its [Natural Sounds Sound Gallery](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm). BIRDCHA streams each recording directly from the National Park Service.

| Common Raven | [Grand Teton National Park](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-common-raven.htm) |
| Hermit Thrush | [Yosemite National Park](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-hermit-thrush.htm) |
